# Limitations

This project is intended to support practical thinking, research synthesis, and implementation planning. It is not a substitute for qualified professional advice.

## General limitations

- This project does not provide legal, clinical, medical, regulatory, financial, HR, or professional advice.
- Recommendations are intended as evidence-to-action support, not instructions that should be followed without local judgement.
- Organisations should validate any proposed workflow change against their own operational, legal, security, data protection, and governance requirements.
- Evidence quality, source age, and applicability may vary by sector and use case.
- Public sources may omit implementation detail, negative results, or operational constraints.

## AI-specific limitations

- AI outputs can be incomplete, misleading, or wrong.
- AI-generated summaries must be checked against original sources before being used as evidence.
- AI should not make or automate high-stakes decisions without accountable human review.
- AI should not be used to hide uncertainty, weak evidence, or process risk.
- AI tools may process data in ways that are unsuitable for sensitive, personal, confidential, clinical, financial, or regulated information unless appropriate controls are in place.

## Sector-specific limitations

Industry appendices are implementation translations of the core research. They are not independent expert guidance.

Particular caution is required for:

- Healthcare and clinical operations.
- Veterinary clinical operations.
- Public services.
- Legal, financial, or regulated workflows.
- Any process involving vulnerable people, safety-critical judgement, or sensitive personal data.

No medical, clinical, veterinary, legal, financial, or regulatory recommendation should be treated as authoritative without qualified review.

## Evidence limitations

Each source should be interpreted according to:

- Source type.
- Method quality.
- Publication date.
- Publisher credibility.
- Relevance to the research question.
- Whether the claim is general or sector-specific.
- Whether the source is independent or vendor-produced.

Evidence strength should be recorded in `evidence-register.csv`.

## Implementation limitations

Reducing admin burden is not just a tooling problem. Many failures come from unclear ownership, poor process design, weak data quality, over-automation, bad incentives, and lack of review discipline.

Automation should not be used to accelerate a broken process without first understanding the work.

## Publication rule

If a claim cannot be supported, it should be softened, marked as judgement, moved into a hypothesis section, or removed.
