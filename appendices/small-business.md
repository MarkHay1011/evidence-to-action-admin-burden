# Appendix: Small Owner-Led Businesses

**Status:** implementation appendix stub.

This appendix translates the core research into practical implementation patterns for small owner-led businesses.

## Typical admin-burden patterns

- Enquiries spread across email, web forms, phone, social media, and WhatsApp.
- Manual follow-up and quote chasing.
- Spreadsheet-based job, customer, stock, or event tracking.
- Repeated copy/paste between forms, email, invoices, and calendars.
- Limited weekly visibility of pipeline, workload, cash, or profitability.
- Owner memory acting as the operating system, which is thrilling in the same way a cliff edge is scenic.

## Candidate low-risk interventions

- Web form to structured enquiry register.
- Email enquiry triage and draft response.
- Simple customer follow-up sequence.
- Weekly pipeline/status snapshot.
- Event/job profitability tracker.
- Customer feedback theme summary.
- Spreadsheet cleanup and dashboard starter pack.

## Governance considerations

- Keep owner approval for customer-facing messages.
- Do not automate pricing, contractual promises, or sensitive decisions without review.
- Keep data handling simple and explicit.
- Prefer visible logs over hidden automations.

## First useful pilot

Map one repeat workflow, such as enquiry to quote to follow-up, then test a small intervention that saves time without changing the customer promise.
