# Appendix: Charities and Community Organisations

**Status:** implementation appendix stub.

This appendix translates the core research into practical implementation patterns for charities, community groups, and small mission-led organisations.

## Typical admin-burden patterns

- Volunteer coordination across email, chat, spreadsheets, and forms.
- Grant, funding, and reporting administration.
- Repeated beneficiary, donor, or stakeholder communication.
- Manual event coordination.
- Policy and safeguarding document lookup.
- Evidence gathering for impact reporting.
- Limited capacity for structured process improvement.

## Candidate lower-risk interventions

- Volunteer enquiry to structured register.
- Meeting notes to action log.
- Funding opportunity tracker.
- Drafting routine updates for human review.
- Impact evidence collection template.
- Policy lookup with source links.
- Weekly operations snapshot.

## High-risk areas requiring specialist review

- Safeguarding decisions.
- Eligibility or access decisions.
- Advice to vulnerable people.
- Sensitive personal data handling.
- Fundraising, legal, or regulatory commitments.

## Governance considerations

- Keep decisions affecting people with accountable humans.
- Separate drafting support from decision-making.
- Use clear review logs.
- Protect sensitive beneficiary and volunteer data.
- Avoid automation that creates a false sense of capacity.
