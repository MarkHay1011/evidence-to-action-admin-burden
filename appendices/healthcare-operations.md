# Appendix: Healthcare Operations

**Status:** implementation appendix stub.

This appendix is about operational and administrative burden in healthcare settings. It is not clinical guidance.

## Boundary

Do not use this appendix to make clinical recommendations, triage decisions, diagnosis suggestions, treatment recommendations, or patient-specific advice.

Any healthcare implementation should be reviewed by qualified operational, clinical, information governance, and regulatory stakeholders.

## Typical admin-burden patterns

- Repeated documentation.
- Manual referral or booking coordination.
- Status chasing across teams.
- Inbox-driven work queues.
- Duplicated data entry.
- Report preparation and validation.
- Policy or process lookup.
- Handoff friction between administrative and clinical teams.

## Candidate lower-risk interventions

- Drafting non-clinical administrative messages for human review.
- Summarising operational meeting notes into action logs.
- Creating structured task lists from non-clinical workflows.
- Improving operational reporting and exception visibility.
- Supporting policy lookup with clear source links.
- Mapping administrative process bottlenecks.

## High-risk areas requiring specialist review

- Clinical triage.
- Diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Patient-specific recommendations.
- Safety-critical escalation.
- Eligibility decisions.
- Any use of sensitive patient data.

## Governance considerations

- Clear accountability.
- Human review of outputs.
- Information governance controls.
- Source traceability.
- Audit logs.
- Explicit exclusion of clinical decision-making unless governed by qualified teams.

## Evidence required

- Healthcare admin burden research.
- Evidence on documentation burden.
- Operational improvement studies.
- AI governance and health data regulation sources.
- Examples of safe administrative automation.
