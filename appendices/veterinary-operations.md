# Appendix: Veterinary Operations

**Status:** implementation appendix stub.

This appendix is about operational and administrative burden in veterinary settings. It is not veterinary clinical guidance.

## Boundary

Do not use this appendix to make clinical recommendations, diagnosis suggestions, treatment recommendations, triage decisions, or patient-specific advice.

Any veterinary implementation should be reviewed by qualified operational, clinical, information governance, and regulatory stakeholders.

## Typical admin-burden patterns

- Call, booking, and enquiry handling.
- Repeated client communication.
- Manual case-status coordination.
- Handoff friction between front-of-house, clinical, and operational teams.
- Reporting burden across clinics or services.
- Rota, staffing, and capacity visibility gaps.
- Rework caused by inconsistent categorisation or incomplete records.

## Candidate lower-risk interventions

- Non-clinical message drafting for human review.
- Operational meeting summaries and action logs.
- Admin workflow mapping.
- Reporting QA and exception lists.
- Staffing or capacity visibility templates.
- Source-backed process documentation support.

## High-risk areas requiring specialist review

- Clinical triage.
- Diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Patient-specific recommendations.
- Emergency prioritisation.
- Clinical governance decisions.
- Any use of sensitive client, colleague, or patient data.

## Governance considerations

- Keep clinical judgement with qualified professionals.
- Separate operational analytics from clinical advice.
- Make data definitions explicit.
- Document fallback logic and known data-quality constraints.
- Ensure human review before operational recommendations are acted on.

## Evidence required

- Veterinary operational workload sources.
- Admin burden and documentation sources.
- Evidence on staffing, capacity, communication, or reporting friction.
- Relevant governance and professional standards sources.
