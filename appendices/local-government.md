# Appendix: Local Government and Public Services

**Status:** implementation appendix stub.

This appendix translates the core research into practical implementation patterns for local government and public-service operations. It is not legal, policy, regulatory, or statutory guidance.

## Typical admin-burden patterns

- Case, enquiry, and request handling across multiple channels.
- Manual status updates and citizen follow-up.
- Duplicated data entry between legacy systems.
- Policy lookup and interpretation support needs.
- Reporting and statutory return preparation.
- Backlog visibility gaps.
- Handoffs across departments, contractors, or partner organisations.

## Candidate lower-risk interventions

- Service request triage support for human review.
- Standard response drafting with source-controlled wording.
- Meeting and case-note summarisation where policy permits.
- Backlog and exception reporting.
- Policy lookup with source links and version dates.
- Admin-burden mapping across high-volume workflows.

## High-risk areas requiring specialist review

- Eligibility decisions.
- Statutory decisions.
- Legal interpretation.
- Benefits, housing, safeguarding, enforcement, or social-care decisions.
- Citizen-facing advice where errors could materially affect access or rights.
- Sensitive personal data handling.

## Governance considerations

- Accountability must remain with the public body and named decision owners.
- AI use should be transparent where it affects public-facing outputs.
- Source material should be version-controlled where possible.
- Human review must be explicit for citizen-impacting work.
- Equality, bias, accessibility, and data protection considerations should be reviewed before scaling.
